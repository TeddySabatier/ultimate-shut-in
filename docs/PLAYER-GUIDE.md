# Haven Protocol: player guide

Haven Protocol turns real-life habits into a small fortress-building game. You do something in the real world, you tap Done, and your Haven grows. This guide explains everything the game does, in plain words.

**The one promise: the Haven is always safe.** Nothing you have earned can be taken away. A wave cannot breach the walls, a utility running low costs nothing, and a vassal who has gone quiet can be brought back in one tap.

## First launch

A short three-step setup opens the first time:

1. **Protocol online.** A few lines of story. Tap Skip to read it instantly.
2. **Register the Absolute.** Choose a name (up to 24 characters, default "Absolute").
3. **Assign the vassals.** Pick which of the eight habits you want and a difficulty. Everything can be changed later in Settings.

Then your first board is issued. You can install the game to your home screen: on Android Chrome use "Install app" from the menu, on iPhone use Safari, Share, "Add to Home Screen". It then works offline.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| **Haven** | The ring scene, Domain Points progress, the three utilities, the wave countdown, your vassals and a short recent log. A *Map* chip in the header jumps to the City Map. |
| **Board** | Today's quests and how long until the day resets. |
| **Map** | The City Map: the seven zones around the Haven, the wall creeping outward with every Domain Point, your outposts, last week's wall and the marks the wave left. |
| **Ledger** | Level, settlement, titles, vassal details, forged combo items, wave history with replayable battle logs, lifetime stats, story vignettes and (when relevant) the Resurrection token. |
| **Settings** | Name, difficulty, benevolence, enabled habits, reset hour, reduced motion, export/import, reset. |

## The eight vassals

Each habit has one vassal. Completing a quest of that habit gives that vassal the same XP you get, and it levels up (1 to 30).

| Habit | Vassal | What it tracks | Utility it feeds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Pell the Runner | Exercise and movement: walks, stairs, workouts, dancing in the kitchen | none |
| Outside | Wren the Scout | Daylight and time outdoors: doorstep air, walks, errands on foot, parks | none |
| Clean | Ondra the Steward | Tidying and chores: beds, dishes, surfaces, laundry, floors | none |
| Social | Tobi the Herald | Contact with people: messages, calls, meeting up, saying thanks | none |
| Study | Imre the Archivist | Learning and deep work: reading, focus blocks, lessons, practice | none |
| Sleep | Lunel the Nightwatch | Lights-out and wake times, wind-down, screens off, enough hours | Power |
| Eat | Basil the Quartermaster | Meals, cooking, groceries and water (never calories or weight) | Gas |
| Hygiene | Marisol the Bathkeeper | Self-care: teeth, showers, fresh clothes, grooming, appointments | Water |

Each vassal has three numbers:

- **Level** (1 to 30): the permanent record of time spent together. It adds to your wave defense.
- **Trust** (0 to 7 pips): the days this week the vassal had at least one quest Done or Tried. Resets after each wave.
- **Loyalty** (0 to 100): starts at 50. Rises with each completed quest, drifts down after days of neglect. Neglected vassals are more likely to get a quest on your board.

You can disable habits you do not want in Settings. A disabled habit gets no quests, and its vassal and utility are frozen (nothing decays, nothing counts toward the wave).

## Difficulty

Pick a pace in Settings. A change applies from the next board.

| | Gentle | Steady (default) | Hardcore |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quests per day | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Mix of quest sizes | mostly small | a mix | mostly big |
| XP and coin multiplier | x1.00 | x1.10 | x1.20 |
| Loyalty gained per quest | +8 | +6 | +5 |
| Loyalty lost per idle day | -2 | -3 | -5 |
| Utility lost per day without its habit | -10 | -15 | -20 |
| Wave strength | x0.80 | x1.00 | x1.25 |

Hardcore reaches new rings roughly twice as fast as Gentle. Gentle is a perfectly good way to play for years.

## The board

Each game day (it starts at 04:00 by default, so a quest done at 01:30 still belongs to the day before) the Protocol issues a board of 3 to 5 quests, drawn from your enabled habits at a size that fits your ring and difficulty. The board is the same if you reopen the app; it only changes at the reset.

There are three kinds of quest:

- **Check.** Do it, tap **Done**.
- **Timer.** Tap **Start timer** if you want a stopwatch. Stop and resume as often as you like; it survives closing the app. Running it for the full suggested time gives +10% XP. The timer is friction, never a gate: you can tap Done at any time.
- **Count.** Use the minus and plus buttons. Reaching the target completes the quest automatically (with an Undo). Counting past the target earns nothing extra.

Other things on the board:

- **Bonus contract.** The biggest quest of the day is a bonus: double XP, coins and Domain Points.
- **I tried.** If you attempted something and could not finish, tap **I tried**. It counts for trust and keeps loyalty from fading that day, nothing else. You can still upgrade it to Done later the same day.
- **Undo.** Tapped Done by mistake? Undo reverses the exact rewards, but only until the day resets. Titles you already earned are never taken back.
- **Swap quest.** Once a day you can swap one open quest for another of the same habit (and the same size where possible) for 15 coins (10 from The Block). If you swap the bonus quest itself, the bonus moves to the biggest quest still open.

The quests are self-reported. The game never checks a sensor, photo or location, and it never assumes you are cheating.

## Settlement: what a quest pays

Each completed quest pays out in a short "Settlement" report:

- **XP** for you and for that habit's vassal.
- **Coins** ("settlement"). Spent only on optional things (swaps and the Resurrection token). They simply accumulate otherwise.
- **Domain Points (DP)**: the quest's size (1, 2 or 3), doubled on the bonus contract. DP never decrease and push the ring boundary outward.
- **Loyalty** for the vassal.
- If it feeds a utility, **power, water or gas** is restored.

Your own level (1 to 50) shows in the Ledger and is a milestone; the ring is the real progression.

## Utilities

Power (fed by Sleep), Water (Hygiene) and Gas (Eat) each run from 0 to 100 and start full. Completing a quest of the tied habit restores 25 (40 for a big one). At each day reset, if you did not do the tied habit that day, the utility drops by the amount in the difficulty table. States are Bright (70 and up), Flickering, Dim and Out, and the Haven picture changes to match.

The only consequence is good news: if all three are at 70 or more when the wave settles you get a **Well-kept Haven** defense bonus (+5, or +8 from The Block). An Out utility costs you nothing. If you disable a habit, its utility is frozen and hidden.

## The weekly wave

Every 7 days from your first day, a wave of monsters (Dust Bunny, Doomscroll Wyrm, the Static every tenth week...) attacks. It is settled the next time you open the app after the week ends, and the Haven tab counts down to it.

**Defense** is made of:

- each vassal's level plus twice their trust this week,
- your ring's wall bonus,
- the combo items you forged this week (at most 6 count, strongest first),
- title bonuses (flat defense, capped at +12),
- the Well-kept Haven bonus.

**Result** compares defense to the wave's attack (which grows with the week number up to week 52, with your ring and with difficulty):

| Result | When | Domain Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decisive | defense is at least 1.5 times the attack | +22 |
| Win | defense at least equals the attack | +15 |
| Held | defense is lower | +5, "the walls held" |
| Quiet | you had zero trust all week (you were away) | 0, nothing happens |

A "Held" week takes nothing away. Trust and combo items reset after each wave, and a replayable battle log is saved in the Ledger.

## Rings

Domain Points move you outward through seven rings. Crossing a threshold plays a short story vignette and unlocks facilities.

| Ring | Name | DP needed | Walls | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The Room | 0 | +5 | Quest Board, Ledger |
| 2 | The Flat | 120 | +8 | Kitchen (tier 2 Eat quests, Resupply run); Improvised Forge (combos start forging); Hallway Noticeboard (more tier 2 quests for every habit) |
| 3 | The Floor | 330 | +11 | Stairwell Gym (tier 3 Move quests); Reading Nook (more Study quests); Linen Room (tier 3 Hygiene and Clean quests) |
| 4 | The Building | 650 | +14 | Rooftop (more Outside and Social quests); Common Room (Join the crowd, a Social quest); Workshop (combo items get +1 power) |
| 5 | The Block | 1,100 | +17 | Corner Shop (Resurrection token 450 instead of 500, swap 10 instead of 15); Courtyard (Well-kept bonus +8) |
| 6 | The District | 1,700 | +20 | District Clinic (utilities fade 25% slower); District Library (title XP bonuses can stack to +25% instead of +20%) |
| 7 | The City | 2,500 | +23 | Observatory (the map shows the whole city at night); Open Sky (nothing further to unlock; waves and DP continue) |

Rings never go down. Even undoing a quest leaves a ring you already reached.

## The City Map and outposts

The **Map** tab turns the Domain Points number into territory: a small isometric city at night, seven zones around the Haven tower, one per ring. The amber dashed **wall** is your boundary. It does not jump once per ring: it creeps outward with every Domain Point, up to 85% of the way to the next zone, and crosses when the ring unlocks. Buildings inside the wall light up; the zone just beyond it wakes up ("dawn") as the wall gets close; everything further out sits in static, so you can always see the shape of what is coming. The map is not geographic and never asks where you are.

- **Tap a zone or a building** for its sheet: status (claimed, current, next with the DP still to go, or beyond the wall), the facilities it holds, the outposts in it and its story vignette (claimed zones can replay it). On a keyboard, Tab reaches seven zone bands and every pin; Enter opens, Escape closes.
- **7 days** draws last week's wall as a dashed ghost and hatches the new ground: "+78 DP in 7 days. The wall moved 0.4 streets." One cell is one street.
- **List** swaps the map for seven zone rows with their outposts; everything on the map can be done from the list. The choice is remembered on this device.
- **Since your last look**: opening the tab animates the wall from where it stood last time and shows the DP you gained meanwhile.
- **Walls held**: every settled wave, won or lost, leaves scorch marks on the wall as it stood that week (fading over two weeks) and a shield flag for seven days. The Domain window counts them.

**Outposts** are real places you keep going back to: a gym, a park, a library, a friend's door. *Mark an outpost* asks for a name, a type, the related habits (one or two), how far it feels (a zone, beyond the wall included) and which way; nothing else, no address, no GPS. Then log visits:

- **I was there**: one tap on the outpost's row or sheet. Ten minutes on a park bench counts.
- **Where were you?** After a quest settles, the toast offers up to three related outposts. One tap tags the quest; it never blocks anything.
- A custom quest (Compose, "Where does it happen?") can default to an outpost, so a "gym session" quest tags itself.

**One counted visit per outpost per game day**, whichever route. Visits pay nothing by themselves; the states do:

| State | Visits | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marked | 0 | It is on the map. |
| Scouted | 3 | Swapping a quest of a related habit costs 5 less settlement (so 10 instead of 15; 5 with the Corner Shop). |
| Corridor | 10 | Quests of related habits return +10% settlement, and the wall reaches out to the outpost on the map. |
| Stronghold | 30 | +2 defense in every weekly wave (at most +6 across all strongholds) and a small Haven annex stands on the map. |

States never drop, and perks are per habit with the best outpost winning (two Scouted gyms give one discount, not two). An outpost with no visit for 14 days (7 if never visited) is shown as **quiet**: dimmed, with a "zz". Nothing fades; one visit wakes it.

Caps: **3 + your ring** active outposts (4 at The Room, 10 at The City), at most 3 per zone, and pins keep three cells apart. **Retire** an outpost from its sheet when you stop going; it leaves the map and keeps its visits in the Ledger, where you can **Restore** it (if there is a free slot) or, for privacy, **Forget** it, which deletes it and its visits after you type FORGET.

## Resurrection token

An optional, rare "undo for a bad stretch". It costs 500 coins (450 from The Block) and can be used once per calendar month. It restores **one** thing lost in the last 14 days:

- a day-in-a-row run that ended (the count is restored and the gap is filled in), or
- a vassal who left in Strict mode (returns at loyalty 50).

The Ledger only shows the token when there is something to restore, so the game never advertises losing. Using it earns the Returned title.

## Benevolent and Strict modes

- **Benevolent** (default): loyalty never goes below 1. A neglected vassal just "waits" and nobody ever leaves.
- **Strict**: if a vassal's loyalty reaches 0 they take leave: their quests stop appearing and their card greys out. Tap **Welcome back** (free) and they return at loyalty 50 with their level and XP intact, and you earn the Open Door title the first time.

Switching from Strict to Benevolent brings every away vassal back at once. Switching to Strict changes nothing else.

## Missing days

Life happens. The game is built so that being away never snowballs:

- Each vassal has one **free idle day** before loyalty starts to fade.
- However long you are gone, loyalty fades **at most 7 times** per vassal in one return, and each utility drops for **at most 3 days**.
- **No boards are generated** for the days you missed, and missed days are not counted as losses.
- A wave week in which you did nothing at all is **Quiet**: no attack, no Domain Points, no log. A week with any activity is settled normally.
- The only thing a gap resets is the in-a-row day count (shown only in the Ledger), and the Resurrection token can restore it.
- You get one "welcome back" line on the Haven tab, nothing more.

Example: a Steady player gone for 30 days with every vassal at loyalty 60 comes back to loyalty 39, utilities down at most 45 points, a few quiet waves and a fresh board. Nothing is lost.

## Reminders and app shortcuts

Settings has a **Reminders** window. It is off until you switch on **Send me reminders**; that tap is the only moment the browser is asked for permission to show notifications. If you say no, nothing is sent, and the window explains how to allow it again in the browser's site settings.

Once on, there are three reminders, each with its own toggle:

| Reminder | When | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Board issued** | Every day at the time you choose, never before your day starts. | 08:30 |
| **Wave tomorrow** | The calendar day before a wave settles (the wave week ends at your day-start hour). | 19:00 |
| **Timer finished** | The moment a running timer quest reaches its time, if it is still the same day's board. | on |

They read like a system window: the title is "SYSTEM", then one warm line. Nothing is sent between your **Lights out** and **Wake** hours (Settings, Hours): a board reminder waits for the wake hour, the other two are skipped.

**What reminders cannot do.** There is no server and no push service, so reminders are scheduled by the app on your device and only arrive **while Haven Protocol is open on screen, or was left open a moment ago**: phones pause apps in the background. When the app is closed nothing fires, and a reminder missed while your phone slept is not sent later. Tapping a reminder opens the app on the right screen (the Board for a board or timer reminder, the Haven for a wave). The choices are kept on this device only (not in the save file, so they are not exported), and if the browser will not store them the switch stays off with a note.

**App shortcuts.** After you install the app, long-press its icon (Android) or right-click it (desktop) for three shortcuts: **Add my own quest** (opens the Board with the compose sheet ready), **Today's board** and **Map**.

## Export and import

Settings has **Export save** (downloads a `haven-save-YYYY-MM-DD.json` file) and **Import save** (pick a file, confirm; it replaces everything in this Haven). This is the only way to move a Haven between devices or browsers, and a good habit before clearing browser data. Older saves are upgraded automatically on import.

## FAQ

**Is it honest? Can I just tap Done?**
You can. Quests are self-reported on the honor system, and there is nothing to win by cheating: the rewards are small and the game never implies suspicion. The point is that you know.

**Is my data uploaded anywhere?**
No. There is no server, no account and no analytics. Your Haven lives in your browser's local storage on your own device. The only outside request is the fonts from Google Fonts. Because nothing is synced, use Export save if you want a backup or a second device.

**Can I lose the Haven?**
Not through play. Waves cannot breach it, utilities cannot cause harm, and vassals can always be welcomed back. The ways to lose it are your own: pressing **Reset everything** in Settings (it asks you to confirm), or clearing the browser's site data without an export.

**What time does the day change?**
At the "Day starts at" hour in Settings (04:00 by default). Changing it keeps today's board and moves the reset to the next time that hour comes round. Days around a daylight-saving change can last 23 or 25 hours.

**Does it send notifications?**
No. The app never reaches out; you open it when you want to.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes, once loaded and installed. The game runs entirely on your device.

**Why is there a "wave" if losing is impossible?**
It is the weekly beat that turns a week of effort into Domain Points and a story moment. A harder week just pays a little less.

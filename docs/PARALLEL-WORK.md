## Stream A — SVG budget and map-component tests

Owner files:

- `src/components/map/layout.ts`
- `src/components/map/CityMapSvg.tsx`
- `src/components/map/CityMap.module.css`
- `src/components/map/CityMapSvg.test.tsx`

`CityMapSvg` worst case serialises to 74,971 characters. The budget in CITY-MAP 5.4 is under 60,000 characters and under 1,500 elements, one radial gradient, at most 16 patterns, no `<image>`, `<filter>`, or `data-*`.

Shrink the markup. Measure which layer costs the bytes before cutting. Keep the grammar: claimed, dawn, corridor, and fog tiles; the creeping wall, ghost, and residue; pins; seven zone bands. Ground tiles should stay `<use>`s of shared symbols. Integer anchors, one decimal on deltas.

Two test fixtures are wrong and must be fixed in `CityMapSvg.test.tsx` without weakening the budget:

- `getByRole('button', { name: /Place op1/ })` also matches "Place op10". Anchor the pattern (`/^Place op1,/`, `/^Place op7,/`).
- Dawn: cells are integer. Zone 2 ends at radius 1.62 and the nearest zone-3 cell is distance 2, so `wallR` 1.9 covers no dawn cell. Use a radius that does (2.05). `.g.s-c` stays 9 for ring 2. Do not change `tileState` to satisfy the old radius.

## Stream B — Map screen tests

- `src/screens/MapScreen.test.tsx`

`getByText('Outposts', { selector: 'span' })` matches the Domain stat and the Outposts window title. Assert the stat (`span.statKey`) without dropping the window title.

Do not change player-facing copy or components. The screen behaviour under test is already correct.
